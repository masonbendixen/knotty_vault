## 1. Positioning (the thing all the ads should ladder up to)

Before tactics, a quick frame. The four focus classes — **strength & conditioning, aerial, handstand, partner acro** — are not really "yoga." They share a different promise:

- **You will do something that looks impossible.** That's the hook. People don't scroll-stop for "get fit"; they stop for "wait, *I* could do that?"
- **It's playful and social, not grind-y.** Partner acro and aerial especially are about trust, play, and connection — a counter-position to the lonely gym treadmill.
- **It's a skill, not a workout.** You leave with a *thing you can do*, and progress is visible and addictive.

A working tagline for the brand umbrella (not an ad slogan, the north star):
**"Strength you can show off."** or **"Train for the impossible."**

Everything below should feel like it belongs to that promise.

## 2. Video content ideas for ads

Your partner-acro "couple's counseling" concept is genuinely great — it has a *reveal* and a *reframe*, which is exactly what short-form needs. Here are more in that vein, grouped by the mechanic that makes them work.

### A. The Reveal (start tight, pull back to a surprise) — your couple's-counseling pattern
- **"It's like couples counseling… but stronger."** (your idea) Close on two faces almost touching → pan out → they're stacked on a flying pose on a base → cut to dynamic washing-machine / standing-hand-to-hand. Caption sells the joke, the move sells the studio.
- **Handstand "morning coffee"**: tight on someone calmly sipping coffee → pull back, they're in a one-arm handstand on the counter, coffee in the other hand. "Mondays hit different."
- **Aerial "I needed space"**: tight on someone saying/text-overlay "I told my therapist I needed to feel weightless" → reveal them unspooling into a silks drop.
- **The "office chair" cut**: someone slumped at a desk → smash cut to same person inverted on the silks / pressing to handstand → "There's a better way to spend 6pm."

### B. The Transformation / Time-lapse (progress is the product)
- **"Day 1 vs Day 90"** split-screen of a real beginner — wobbly first chin-stand → clean handstand. Authenticity > polish here. This is the single highest-converting format for skill-based studios.
- **"Everyone said I was too old / too weak / too uncoordinated"** text-on-screen over a beginner's journey montage. Identity + objection-handling in one.

### C. The Trust / Connection angle (partner acro & community)
- **"Trust falls, but make it athletic."** Quick montage of catches, counterbalances, the moment of leaving the ground.
- **Date-night reframe**: "Cheaper than dinner. Better than a movie. You'll actually talk to each other." Couples doing partner acro, laughing.
- **Friendship reframe**: a group counterbalance circle collapsing into laughter — "find your people."

### D. The "Satisfying" / ASMR-adjacent loop (pure scroll-stop, no narrative)
- Slow-mo chalk clap before a handstand. Silks unfurling. The clean *clack* of a hand-to-hand entry. These do nothing but earn the watch-time the algorithm rewards; pair with a simple "Beginners welcome. [Studio]" end card.

### E. The Humor / Relatable angle
- **"POV: your first aerial class"** — exaggerated terror → unexpected grace → ear-to-ear grin.
- **"Things my non-acro friends say"** — quick-cut bits ("aren't you scared?", "isn't that just yoga?") answered with a move.
- **Bloopers / fails montage** — humanizing, shareable, makes it feel approachable rather than elite.

### Production notes
- Shoot **vertical 9:16 first**, everything else is a crop.
- First **0.8 seconds must contain motion or a face** — no logo intros.
- Capture **B-roll in bulk**: one good shoot can yield 20+ clips. Get slow-mo, wide, tight, entrances, the *reaction/laugh*, and the *mess-up-then-get-it*.
- Always end with a **clear next step**: "First class [free / $X]. Link in bio." An ad with no CTA is a branding exercise you can't afford yet.

## 3. Photo ideas

- **The "impossible silhouette"**: a clean handstand or aerial shape against a bright, simple background (window light, colored wall). These are your billboard / profile-grid anchors.
- **Connection close-ups**: hands gripping wrists, the catch, eye contact mid-pose. Sells the emotional benefit, not just the athletic one.
- **Real-people-mid-laugh**: the antidote to intimidating fitness photography. Critical for "beginners welcome" messaging.
- **Texture/detail**: chalked hands, silks bunched in a grip, bare feet on a base's feet. Great for variety in the grid and for ad backgrounds.
- **Before/after-feel portraits**: confident posture, direct gaze — "I'm someone who does this now."
- **Environmental wide shots** of the space itself — for Google Business Profile and "what does the studio look like" trust-building.

Tip: shoot a **bright/clean set** AND a **moody/dramatic set** in the same session. Bright converts better in ads (reads as friendly/accessible); moody performs well for brand prestige and merch.

## 5. Monthly budget plans

A few principles baked into all tiers:
- **Don't split a tiny budget across many platforms.** Below ~$15/day a platform can't exit its learning phase. Concentrate.
- **Meta (Instagram + Facebook) is one ad account, one budget** — let Meta distribute across both placements. Don't think of IG and FB as separate spends.
- **Google "Search" is intent capture** (people already searching "aerial class near me"); **Meta is demand creation** (people who didn't know they wanted it). For a novel/visual offering, **Meta is the workhorse**; Google Search is a cheap always-on safety net.
- **Always reserve a slice for "boosting your single best organic post"** — your own winning Reel outperforms most purpose-built ads.
- **Free/owned channels do the heavy lifting at every tier** (Section 6). Paid amplifies what's already working.

| Tier | Meta (IG+FB) ads | Google | Boost best organic post | Other / experiment | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **$100/mo** | $80 (80%) | $0 | $20 (20%) | $0 | One platform only. ~$2.65/day Meta — keep ONE simple campaign, one audience, one CTA. Lean hard on free channels. |
| **$200/mo** | $140 (70%) | $20 (10%) Google Business Profile is free; this is a tiny Search safety net for "aerial class near me" | $40 (20%) | $0 | Now you can run 2 creatives and let Meta pick a winner. |
| **$400/mo** | $260 (65%) | $60 (15%) Search | $40 (10%) | $40 (10%) — first Reels/TikTok test or a local micro-influencer trade | Enough to A/B test audiences AND creatives. |
| **$500/mo** | $300 (60%) | $75 (15%) Search + first retargeting | $50 (10%) | $75 (15%) — retargeting (warm audiences) + creator collab | Add a **retargeting** layer: cheap, high-ROI, re-touches people who watched 50%+ of a video or hit the site. |

**The single most important spend rule for a small budget:** put real money into **one clear offer** (e.g., "$X intro class" or "free first class"), point all ads at it, and measure cost-per-signup — not likes. Likes don't pay rent; bums on mats do.

## 6. Free / low-cost channels (do these regardless of budget)

- **Google Business Profile** — free, and the #1 thing for "near me" searches. Photos, hours, class list, reviews. Ask every happy student for a review.
- **Instagram + TikTok organic** — post the same vertical clips you're already making for ads. 4–7x/week. Reels reach > feed by a mile right now.
- **Meetup.com** — a "Partner Acro Jam" or "Handstand Practice" group is *perfect* for this niche; it's how a huge share of the acro community actually finds classes. Low effort, high intent.
- **Facebook Group** (your own community) + posting in **local fitness / dance / circus / "things to do in [city]" groups**.
- **Free community jams / "bring a friend" days** — partner acro especially spreads by people physically bringing someone.
- **Cross-promotion / trade** with nearby complementary businesses (climbing gyms, dance studios, physiotherapists, the future spa/massage side of your own business).
- **Local micro-influencers** — trade free classes for content. One enthusiastic local creator with 5–20k followers often beats paid reach.
- **Referral program** — "bring a friend, you both get a free class." Cheapest acquisition there is.
- **Email/SMS list** from day one — capture every lead; it's the only audience you actually own.

## 7. Suggested sequence (how I'd roll this out)

1. **One photo + video shoot** to bank 6–8 photos and 20+ vertical clips.
2. **Set up the free foundation**: Google Business Profile, IG/TikTok, Meetup group, email capture.
3. **Define the offer** (intro deal) and a simple landing/booking page to send ads to.
4. **Post organically for 2–3 weeks**, see which clips pop.
5. **Put paid money behind the winners** at whatever budget tier you pick.
6. **Add retargeting** once there's enough traffic ($400+ tier).
